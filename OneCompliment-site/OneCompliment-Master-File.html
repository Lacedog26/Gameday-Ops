Naming
Name shortlist — decision pending
All "Lift" variants are taken or risky. Best available candidates that appear clear on app stores and USPTO:
Floriment
flor (flower) + iment (compliment). Sounds like "flourishment." Completely original.
Inua
Swahili for "lift up." 4 letters. Warm, global, no app found.
Sunlift
Sun + lift. Says everything instantly. Nobody has it.
Florise
Flor (flower) + rise. French/Latin feel. Appears clear.
Sunward
Sunflowers turn toward the sun daily — that's the habit. Poetic.
Compliora
Compliment + flora. Sophisticated, floral. Appears clear.
Name decided: OneCompliment. File as standard character mark "ONECOMPLIMENT" — no emoji, no sunflower. This is stronger and broader than a stylized mark. Product Decisions
Locked decisions
Things decided and built into the product. Don't revisit unless there's a strong reason.
✅
30 challenges, rotating daily by day of year
Same challenge for every user on earth on the same day. Challenges 1–30 repeat. Self-compliment (challenge 7) kept for variety — one inward day every 10 days is intentional.
✅
☀️ = incomplete, 🌻 = done — always
Consistent visual language throughout the entire app. Never flip these.
✅
Groups are the #1 retention driver
No group = 71% churn. 4+ groups = 6% churn. Onboarding must not let users reach home without joining a group. The paywall goes right after joining the first group.
✅
Free forever for individual users
Wordle model. No paywall for basic use. Revenue comes from Pro upgrades and B2B. The free tier must be genuinely good to drive word of mouth. Free groups: up to 8 members. Pro: unlimited.
✅
Every app update also applies to the browser version
Both files stay in parity at all times. No exceptions.
✅
No Easy/Hard mode — challenges are constraints, not difficulty levels
The constraint IS the difficulty. Adding difficulty labels cheapens the compliment. "Guided vs Open" could unlock at day 14 if added later — but not now.
📱
Full cross-device + dark/light/system mode support
App: useTheme() hook detects system preference and switches between DARK and LIGHT color tokens automatically. Browser: @media (prefers-color-scheme: light) overrides all variables. Both: safe-area-inset for notched phones, -webkit-tap-highlight-color removed, font-size 16px on inputs to prevent iOS zoom, touch-action:manipulation on buttons, prefers-reduced-motion support, tablet/desktop max-width centering, -webkit-font-smoothing for Android/Mac.
🔒
Admin dashboard is password protected
Login gate with password "sunflower2026" (change before going live). Locks for 30 seconds after 5 wrong attempts. Session persists on refresh. Replace with Google OAuth when a developer builds the real backend — only your email address allowed through.
✅
Settings menu with feedback — in app, browser, and org dashboard
⚙️ settings icon in header + bottom nav. Opens a modal with: 5-star rating + written comment (500 chars max). Submit button activates only after a star is selected. On submit shows a 🌻 thank you screen. Feedback goes to your backend/Airtable in production.
✅
3-screen onboarding flow
New users see 3 emotional screens before the home screen: (1) "When's the last time you made someone's day on purpose?" (2) One challenge, every day, under 3 minutes. (3) Do it with people you care about. Progress dots, skip option, localStorage saves completion so it only shows once.
✅
Push notifications — daily reminder
Settings toggle to enable/disable. Time picker defaults to 9:00am. Requests browser notification permission. Schedules a daily reminder at user's chosen time. In production replace with proper push service (OneSignal or Firebase).
✅
Pro features fully implemented in browser version
Streak freeze (1 per week, resets Monday), squad rescue (1 per month, resets 1st), challenge history (last 365 entries), all gated behind isPro() check. Pro activated via ?pro=1 URL param after Stripe payment. Streak now persists to localStorage.
✅
Org custom challenge builder — Team & Enterprise plans
Org admins get a "My Org" tab in the bottom nav. They can create custom challenges with their own prompt, rule, example, and active date range. Custom challenges override the daily challenge for their group. Org plan activated via ?org=classroom/team/enterprise URL param. Access code generated for member invites.
✅
The 🌻 emoji share grid is sacred
This is the organic growth engine. Protect it fiercely. It's worth more than any ad spend. Never put it behind a paywall.
Marketing
Advertising strategy
📱
TikTok is the primary ad channel
Raw, emotional, authentic content only. Best ad: someone filming themselves doing the challenge and the recipient's reaction. Never use polished studio-style ads. Tools: Creatify or Zeely AI for cheap video ad creation.
📸
Instagram Reels is secondary
Slightly more polished. Milestone moments work best here — Day 30 badge, 100-day celebrations.
🎙️
Mid-size creators over celebrities
10k–100k followers in wellness, relationships, self-improvement. Teachers, coaches, pastors, team captains. Authentic beats reach. Give them Pro free for a week and ask them to share genuinely.
🚫
Don't spend on ads until 100 real users
First 100 users = testimonials, streak shares, milestone screenshots. That's your creative library. Then spend $500–$1,000 testing TikTok ads. Scale what converts, kill what doesn't.

Security & Compliance
How OneCompliment protects users
Required reading before launch. Based on 2025 best practices from leading social and payment apps.
Payment Security
🔒
Stripe PCI-DSS Level 1 — highest payment security standard
All payments are processed by Stripe, which holds PCI-DSS Level 1 certification — the highest level available. We never see, store, or touch credit card numbers. Stripe handles all payment data directly in their secure environment. This means we are not a PCI-DSS scope entity as long as we use Stripe Payment Links or Stripe.js. Never build a custom card form that sends card data to your own server.
🔐
HTTPS enforced — all data in transit is encrypted
Netlify automatically provisions SSL/TLS certificates via Let's Encrypt. All data between the user's browser and our servers is encrypted. Never serve the app over HTTP. The .app TLD enforces HTTPS by default in modern browsers — an additional layer of protection.
💳
Stripe webhooks for subscription management
When a real backend is built, use Stripe Webhooks (not just redirect URLs) to verify payment completion. Never grant Pro access based solely on a URL parameter in production — verify server-side via Stripe's API. Current implementation uses ?pro=1 URL param for prototype only.
Contact & User Data Security
📧
Email invites — one-time use, never stored
Email addresses entered for group invitations are used only to send a one-time invitation. They must never be stored in a marketing database, sold, or shared with third parties. In the prototype, emails are stored only in the user's browser localStorage. In production, implement server-side invite tokens that expire after 7 days.
🔑
No phone contact access required
The app uses email-based invites only — no phone contact list access is requested or required. This eliminates the most common privacy complaint in social apps (WhatsApp, Clubhouse, etc. have all faced backlash for harvesting contact lists). Users invite by email or share a link. This is by design.
🗄
Data minimization — only collect what is necessary
Following GDPR Article 5 data minimization principle: collect only name (optional), email (for account), compliments written, group membership, and streak data. No location data, no behavioral profiling, no biometrics. When the backend is built, document every field collected and its legal basis (consent, contract, legitimate interest).
⏱
Data retention limits
Challenge history: retained for 365 days max. Group data: retained until user deletes group. Account data: retained until account deletion + 30 days. Payment records: retained for 7 years (legal/tax requirement). These limits must be documented in the Privacy Policy and technically enforced when the backend is built.
Compliance — CCPA & GDPR
📋
CCPA compliance requirements (California users)
Required if serving California residents (effectively anyone in the US). Must: (1) Disclose what data is collected and why, (2) Provide a "Do Not Sell My Personal Information" link — we don't sell data so this is easy, (3) Honor deletion requests within 45 days, (4) Not discriminate against users who exercise privacy rights. Fine: up to $7,500 per intentional violation. Add a "Privacy" link to every page footer.
🇪🇺
GDPR compliance requirements (EU users)
Required if any EU residents use the app. Must: (1) Get explicit consent before collecting data — the onboarding consent notice covers this, (2) Allow data access, correction, and deletion requests — respond within 30 days, (3) Report data breaches to authorities within 72 hours, (4) Don't transfer EU data outside EU without safeguards (Stripe and Google Analytics are both EU-approved processors). Fine: up to €20 million or 4% of global revenue.
✅
Consent mechanism — built into app
The privacy notice in the group creation form constitutes consent for contact data use. The onboarding flow constitutes consent for app data collection. For full compliance when adding a real backend: add a formal consent checkbox at account creation with a link to Privacy Policy and Terms. Log consent with timestamp, IP address (anonymized), and version of policy accepted.
🍪
Cookie & analytics consent
Google Analytics is currently implemented without a cookie consent banner. For GDPR compliance with EU users, add a cookie consent banner before GA loads. Recommended tool: Cookiebot (free tier available) or build a simple consent gate. For CCPA: add a "Do Not Sell" opt-out. Until this is implemented, consider adding ga('set', 'anonymizeIp', true) to anonymize IPs in GA.
