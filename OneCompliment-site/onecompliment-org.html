How It Works

Simple enough that everyone actually does it.

1
You set up a group
Create a group for your team, class, or organization. Invite members with a link — no app download required.
2
Everyone gets the same daily challenge
Each day there's one constraint: say it in 10 words, say it about a moment, say it out loud. The challenge makes it real.
3
Members write a genuine compliment
Each person chooses someone in their life — a teammate, a student, a coworker — and lifts them up using that day's rule.
4
The group streak grows together
When everyone blooms, the group streak goes up. Milestones at 7, 30, and 100 days. Don't let your squad down.

Why It Works

The constraint is the point.

Anyone can say "good job." The daily challenge forces something more specific, more honest, and more memorable.

🎯 Specificity builds trust. When someone names exactly what you did and why it mattered, it lands completely differently than a generic compliment.
🔥 Streaks create accountability. The group streak means no one wants to be the reason it breaks. Habits form around that.
⏱ Under 3 minutes keeps the bar low. No workshops. No training. Just one genuine thing, once a day.
🌱 Consistency creates culture. A team that lifts each other daily for 30 days is a fundamentally different team than one that doesn't.
